02Features

One field for codes and words
Type 1A00, ba4 or cholera into the same field. Accents are optional, so zawal finds zawał.
Search inside a section
Open a chapter or a block and tap Search here to search only inside it.
The entry, in full
Code, title, definition, coding note, inclusions, exclusions and other names. Every reference to another entry is a link.
ICD-10 to ICD-11
Turn on ICD-10 mapping and type a code such as A01 to get the ICD-11 categories from WHO's official mapping tables. A correspondence is not always one to one, and WHO is not responsible for the mapping.
15 languages and Latin
English plus Arabic, Chinese, Czech, French, German, Kazakh, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish or Uzbek. A missing translation is shown in English, marked EN.
Works offline
English takes about 11 MB, every other language 6 to 8 MB. After the download nothing needs a connection.

03FAQ

Is ICD-11 Global free?

The app is free. After the first weeks it shows a banner at the bottom of the home screen and the chapter list, never on an entry. A one-time purchase removes it; nothing else changes.

Do I need an account?

No. Searches, favorites and history stay on your phone.

What happens when WHO publishes a new release?

The app offers the update.

Are the German and Polish translations final?

No. German is a pre-release translation from WHO, and Polish is the pre-release translation published by the Polish Ministry of Health and Centrum e-Zdrowia. The app labels both as pre-release.

Is this an official WHO app?

No. Not an official WHO product: ICD-11 Global is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHO. It shows the classification as WHO publishes it.

When can I get it?

ICD-11 Global is coming to Google Play and the App Store soon.
