MotoRec

Android · iOS coming soon

Voice recorder with GPS tracking, transcription and car display control.

Made for people on the move. One tap starts recording, and MotoRec keeps going in the background while you ride, drive or walk. Catch a melody before it slips away, narrate a vlog or keep a travel diary on the road.

02Features

Record anywhere
MotoRec runs quietly in the background while you focus on the road.
One tap, no app to open
Start recording from the home screen widget or a Quick Settings tile.
See where you were
With location on, each recording keeps its GPS route. Open the map later and see where you said it.
Transcribe on device
Recordings turn into text with Whisper, right on the phone. Download a speech model once and it works without a connection.
Android Auto
Start recording and play recordings back from your car's display (needs Android Auto developer mode for now).
No account
No sign-in. Your recordings are stored on your device.

03FAQ

Is MotoRec free?

Yes. The free version shows ads, and an optional one-time purchase removes them.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and your recordings are stored on your device.

Does transcription need the internet?

Only once, to download a speech model. After that, recordings are transcribed on the phone without a connection.

Does it work with Android Auto?

Yes. You can control recording and playback from your car's display. For now this needs Android Auto developer mode.

Is there an iOS version?

Not yet. The iPhone version is coming to the App Store soon.
